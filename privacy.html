Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

Nice Pull (nicepull.io) is a free daily sports trivia game. This policy explains what information we collect when you play or create an account, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • If you just play: nothing that identifies you. Your scores and which games you've played are saved in your own browser (local storage) and are not sent to us.
  • If you sign in with Google: Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us, along with a Google account ID. We use these only to create and recognize your Nice Pull account.
  • Your game results, if you're signed in: for each game you finish, we save the game number, the date, your score and title, and your answer to each prompt (the name you entered, the points it scored and how many wrong guesses it took). Scores you earned in the same browser before signing in are copied to your account when you sign in. We use this to show you your stats and to save your history across devices.
  • Technical data: like any website, our hosting and account providers automatically process basic request data (such as IP address, browser type and the time of the visit) to deliver the site, keep it secure and prevent abuse.

We don't run ads, we don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and unlock member features, such as yesterday's game and your stats page.
  • To improve the game, for example by seeing which prompts are too easy or too hard. When we do this, we look at results in aggregate, not at individual players.
  • To run, secure and fix the game.
  • To contact you about your account if we need to (we won't send marketing email without asking first).

Who processes it for us

We use a small number of service providers, who handle data only to provide their service to us:

  • Google: "Sign in with Google," and the site's fonts (Google Fonts).
  • Supabase: stores accounts and handles sign-in.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the site and protects it from attacks.
  • jsDelivr: delivers the code library used for sign-in.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety and rights of our players or of Nice Pull.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information and saved game results for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account information and game results within 30 days, except where we must keep something by law.

Your choices

  • You can play without an account.
  • You can sign out at any time, and you can clear your saved scores by clearing your browser's site data.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing support@nicepull.io. Depending on where you live (for example, Colorado or California), you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, and we will honor requests made under them.
  • You can remove Nice Pull's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account settings, under Security, then "Third-party apps & services."

Children

Nice Pull is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and access to account data is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what we hold.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll also let signed-in players know.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@nicepull.io
